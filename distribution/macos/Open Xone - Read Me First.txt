TL;DR — IF YOUR MAC BLOCKS XONE

First drag Xone to Applications and try opening it once. If macOS blocks it:

• Open Apple menu > System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  The Apple menu is the Apple symbol at the top-left of your screen.
• Scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway next to Xone.
  Confirm with your Mac password or Touch ID, then click Open if asked.

You only need to approve Xone once.

FULL INSTALLATION STEPS

Xone is a free, independently distributed app. This build is not notarized by
Apple, so macOS requires a one-time confirmation before it will open.

1. Drag Xone into the Applications folder.
2. Open Xone from Applications.
3. If macOS says it cannot verify the developer or check the app for malicious
   software, click Done.
4. Open Apple menu > System Settings > Privacy & Security.
5. Scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway next to Xone.
6. Authenticate with your Mac password or Touch ID, then click Open.

You only need to do this once. Xone will open normally afterward.

If Open Anyway is not visible, try opening Xone once more and return to Privacy
& Security. Apple keeps the Open Anyway option available for about one hour
after a blocked launch attempt.

No Terminal command and no change to the Mac's global security settings is
required.

System requirement: Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer).
